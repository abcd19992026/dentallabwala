Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about running Dentivo in your lab.

How does Dentivo help with doctor payments?
Dentivo keeps a running ledger for every doctor account, so balances, payments, and case history stay in one place instead of scattered notebooks or spreadsheets.
Can I print warranty cards from Dentivo?
Yes — generate warranty cards for prosthetic and tooth work using configurable templates, with support for bulk printing and PDF export.
Is Dentivo for a single lab or multiple locations?
Dentivo is designed for dental laboratories managing multiple doctor accounts and cases at volume.
How do I get started?
Log in to your Dentivo account if your lab already has one, or contact the Dentivo team to set up access for your lab.
Is my lab's data secure?
Dentivo is served over an encrypted connection, and access to your lab's data is restricted to authorized accounts.
